# Community conduct

Discuss the work respectfully. Specific technical criticism is welcome; harassment,
threats, discrimination, and personal attacks are not.

Do not publish another person's credentials, private terminal content, contact
details, or identifying information without their consent. Keep issue reports
focused on reproducible behavior.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect contributors.
Use GitHub's reporting tools for abuse. Security vulnerabilities follow the private
reporting guidance in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
